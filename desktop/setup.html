ONE-TIME SETUP

Welcome to Vinyl Room.

Connect your own Spotify developer app to search albums and control playback. Vinyl Room needs only its Client ID.

  1. and select Web API.
  2. Add this exact Redirect URI: http://127.0.0.1:17873/api/spotify/callback
  3. Paste the app's Client ID below. Do not paste a Client Secret.

To play music

Spotify Premium and the Spotify app on this Mac are required. Open Spotify and play a song once so the Mac appears as a device. You can arrange the room without connecting, but search and playback need Spotify.
